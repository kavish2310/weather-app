🧠 Feature Engineering Pipeline

Feature Vector: [Hour of Day, Day of Year, Month, Lagged Humidity, Atmospheric Pressure, Wind Speed, Cloud Cover Fraction]

Normalization: Scikit-learn StandardScaler fitted strictly on chronological training splits to prevent look-ahead bias.

Split Strategy: Non-shuffled 80% chronological train / 20% validation split respecting temporal autocorrelation.

🛡️ Scientific Integrity Principles

• Zero synthetic or randomized weights: Models refuse training until 50+ real observations are collected in the database.

• Outlier Isolation: Isolation Forest isolates multivariate atmospheric extremes (pressure anomalies, microbursts) with contamination α = 0.05.

• Transparent metrics: No claims of 100% predictive accuracy are ever rendered.
