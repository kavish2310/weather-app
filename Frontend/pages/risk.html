How to read this assessment

ℹ️
A score of 0 is a real result. It means the reported live conditions did not cross this model's elevated-risk thresholds; it does not mean data is missing.

“Unavailable” means a required provider measurement was not returned. “Not assessed” means a hazard is not applicable here. This simplified weather assessment is not an official warning or a building-damage prediction.

Physical damage estimate: unavailable without validated local exposure and damage-model data.
